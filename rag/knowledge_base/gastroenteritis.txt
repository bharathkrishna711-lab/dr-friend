# Disease: Gastroenteritis
## Category: Infectious
## Source: NHS - Diarrhoea and Vomiting (nhs.uk)

## Overview
Gastroenteritis is inflammation of the stomach and intestines, usually
caused by a viral or bacterial infection, often linked to contaminated
food or water. It is common and usually resolves on its own within a
few days.

## Common Symptoms
- Loose motion / diarrhoea
- Vomiting and nausea
- Stomach pain or cramps
- Mild fever
- Loss of appetite

## Causes and Risk Factors
- Contaminated food or water (common with street food)
- Viral infections (e.g., norovirus, rotavirus)
- Bacterial infections (e.g., E. coli, Salmonella)
- Poor hand hygiene or sanitation

## Self-Care Guidance
- Stay hydrated with oral rehydration solution or clear fluids
- Eat light, bland foods as tolerated (rice, banana, toast)
- Rest and avoid dairy, caffeine, and fatty foods until recovered
- Maintain good hand hygiene to prevent spread

## When to See a Doctor
- Symptoms lasting more than 3-4 days without improvement
- Signs of dehydration (reduced urination, dizziness, dry mouth)
- High fever above 38.5°C

## When It's an Emergency
- Blood in vomit or stool
- Severe abdominal pain
- Signs of severe dehydration (confusion, very low urine output, rapid heartbeat)
- Persistent vomiting preventing any fluid intake
