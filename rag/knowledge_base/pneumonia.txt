# Disease: Pneumonia
## Category: Respiratory
## Source: WHO Fact Sheet - Pneumonia (who.int)

## Overview
Pneumonia is an infection that inflames the air sacs in one or both lungs,
which may fill with fluid. It can be caused by bacteria, viruses, or fungi,
and ranges from mild to life-threatening, particularly in children, the
elderly, and those with weakened immune systems.

## Common Symptoms
- Cough, often with phlegm (yellow, green, or blood-tinged)
- Fever, sweating, and chills
- Shortness of breath
- Chest pain that worsens with breathing or coughing
- Fatigue and weakness

## Causes and Risk Factors
- Bacterial infection (e.g., Streptococcus pneumoniae)
- Viral infection (including influenza, COVID-19)
- Smoking or chronic lung disease
- Weakened immune system
- Recent respiratory infection that has worsened

## Self-Care Guidance
- Rest and stay well hydrated
- Use fever-reducing medication as needed
- Avoid smoking and secondhand smoke exposure
- Complete any prescribed antibiotic course fully, if bacterial

## When to See a Doctor
- Fever above 38.5°C persisting more than 2 days
- Productive cough with fever lasting more than a few days
- Chest discomfort with breathing

## When It's an Emergency
- Difficulty breathing or rapid breathing
- Oxygen saturation (SpO2) below 93%
- Bluish tint to lips or face
- Confusion or severe drowsiness
- Chest pain with shortness of breath
