# Disease: Typhoid
## Category: Infectious
## Source: WHO Fact Sheet - Typhoid (who.int)

## Overview
Typhoid fever is a bacterial infection caused by Salmonella Typhi, spread
through contaminated food and water. It is common in areas with poor
sanitation and remains a significant health concern in parts of India,
particularly during monsoon season.

## Common Symptoms
- Prolonged high fever, often stepwise increasing over several days
- Headache
- Abdominal pain and bloating
- Loss of appetite
- Weakness and fatigue
- Nausea, sometimes with constipation or diarrhoea

## Causes and Risk Factors
- Consuming contaminated food or water
- Poor sanitation and hygiene practices
- Travel to or residence in endemic areas
- Close contact with an infected person

## Self-Care Guidance
- Rest and stay well hydrated with clean, safe water
- Eat easily digestible, light foods
- Maintain strict hand hygiene to avoid spreading infection
- Complete any prescribed antibiotic course fully

## When to See a Doctor
- Fever persisting more than 3 days without improvement
- Severe abdominal pain
- Persistent headache with high fever

## When It's an Emergency
- Severe abdominal pain with rigidity (possible intestinal complication)
- Confusion or altered consciousness
- Signs of severe dehydration
- Blood in stool

## Typical Lab Findings
- White blood cell count often normal or low (leukopenia), unlike many
  other bacterial infections
- Mildly elevated liver enzymes (SGPT/ALT, SGOT/AST) are common
- Mild anaemia may be present in prolonged illness
- Blood culture positive for Salmonella Typhi confirms diagnosis
