# Disease: Food Poisoning
## Category: Infectious
## Source: NHS - Food Poisoning (nhs.uk)

## Overview
Food poisoning is illness caused by consuming contaminated food or
drink, typically from bacteria, viruses, or toxins. Symptoms usually
appear within hours to a couple of days of eating the affected food and
resolve on their own within a few days.

## Common Symptoms
- Nausea and vomiting
- Diarrhoea
- Stomach cramps
- Mild fever
- Feeling generally unwell
- Loss of appetite

## Causes and Risk Factors
- Undercooked or improperly stored food
- Contaminated water
- Poor food handling hygiene
- Eating food from unreliable street vendors
- Consuming expired or spoiled food

## Self-Care Guidance
- Stay hydrated with oral rehydration solution or clear fluids
- Rest and avoid solid food until nausea subsides, then eat light meals
- Avoid dairy, caffeine, alcohol, and fatty foods until fully recovered
- Practice good hand hygiene to prevent spreading illness

## When to See a Doctor
- Symptoms lasting more than 2-3 days
- Signs of dehydration (reduced urination, dizziness, dry mouth)
- High fever above 38.5°C

## When It's an Emergency
- Blood in vomit or stool
- Severe abdominal pain
- Signs of severe dehydration (confusion, very low urine output,
  rapid heartbeat)
- Persistent vomiting preventing any fluid intake
