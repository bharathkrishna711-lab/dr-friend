# Disease: Hypothyroidism
## Category: Metabolic
## Source: NHS - Underactive Thyroid (nhs.uk)

## Overview
Hypothyroidism occurs when the thyroid gland does not produce enough
thyroid hormone, slowing down the body's metabolism. It develops
gradually and symptoms can be subtle at first, often mistaken for
general tiredness or aging.

## Common Symptoms
- Fatigue and low energy
- Unexplained weight gain
- Feeling cold when others are comfortable
- Dry skin and hair
- Constipation
- Muscle weakness or aches
- Depressed mood
- Slow heart rate

## Causes and Risk Factors
- Autoimmune thyroid disease (most common cause)
- Iodine deficiency
- Previous thyroid surgery or radiation treatment
- Certain medications
- Family history of thyroid disease
- More common in women and with increasing age

## Self-Care Guidance
- Take prescribed thyroid medication consistently, as directed
- Maintain a balanced diet with adequate iodine
- Get regular thyroid function monitoring if diagnosed
- Manage weight through diet and appropriate activity

## When to See a Doctor
- Persistent fatigue, weight gain, or cold intolerance
- Symptoms affecting daily functioning
- Family history of thyroid disease with any symptoms

## When It's an Emergency
- Severe confusion or loss of consciousness (may indicate myxedema
  coma, a rare but serious complication)
- Very slow heart rate with fainting
- Difficulty breathing
