# Disease: Viral Infection
## Category: Respiratory
## Source: NHS - Common Cold and Flu (nhs.uk)

## Overview
A general viral infection (such as the common cold or influenza) affects
the upper respiratory tract and is one of the most frequent reasons
people seek healthcare guidance. Most cases are mild and resolve on
their own within a week to ten days.

## Common Symptoms
- Runny or blocked nose
- Sore throat
- Mild cough
- Sneezing
- Mild fever
- Body aches and fatigue
- Headache

## Causes and Risk Factors
- Viral transmission through respiratory droplets or contact
- Close contact with an infected person
- Weakened immune system
- Seasonal changes, particularly monsoon and winter

## Self-Care Guidance
- Rest and stay well hydrated
- Use steam inhalation or saline nasal rinses for congestion
- Warm fluids and honey for sore throat relief
- Over-the-counter fever/pain relief as needed

## When to See a Doctor
- Symptoms lasting more than 10 days without improvement
- Fever above 38.5°C persisting more than 3 days
- Symptoms significantly worsening after initial improvement

## When It's an Emergency
- Difficulty breathing
- Chest pain
- Confusion or severe drowsiness
- High fever unresponsive to medication
