# Disease: Bronchitis
## Category: Respiratory
## Source: NHS - Bronchitis (nhs.uk)

## Overview
Bronchitis is inflammation of the bronchial tubes that carry air to the
lungs, usually caused by a viral infection. It commonly follows a cold or
flu and typically resolves within a few weeks, though the cough can
persist longer.

## Common Symptoms
- Persistent cough, often producing mucus (clear, white, yellow, or green)
- Chest tightness or mild discomfort
- Mild fever and chills
- Fatigue
- Sore throat
- Wheezing

## Causes and Risk Factors
- Viral infections (most common cause)
- Smoking or exposure to secondhand smoke
- Air pollution or dust exposure
- Weakened immune system
- Recent respiratory infection

## Self-Care Guidance
- Rest and stay well hydrated
- Use a humidifier or steam inhalation to ease congestion
- Avoid smoking and irritants
- Use honey and warm fluids to soothe throat irritation
- Over-the-counter pain relief for fever and discomfort

## When to See a Doctor
- Cough lasting more than 3 weeks
- Fever above 38°C persisting more than 3 days
- Coughing up blood or thick discolored mucus
- Wheezing that worsens

## When It's an Emergency
- Severe difficulty breathing
- Chest pain with breathing difficulty
- Bluish tint to lips or face
- High fever with confusion
