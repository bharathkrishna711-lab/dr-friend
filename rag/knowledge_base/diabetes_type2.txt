# Disease: Type 2 Diabetes
## Category: Metabolic
## Source: WHO Fact Sheet - Diabetes (who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/diabetes)

## Overview
Type 2 diabetes is a chronic condition in which the body does not use insulin
properly, leading to high blood glucose levels over time. It is the most
common form of diabetes and is strongly linked to excess body weight and
physical inactivity. In India, it is one of the most prevalent metabolic
conditions, often developing gradually over years before diagnosis.

## Common Symptoms
- Excessive thirst (polydipsia)
- Frequent urination (polyuria)
- Unexplained fatigue
- Blurred vision
- Slow-healing wounds
- Increased hunger despite eating normally
- Unintended weight loss (in some cases)

## Causes and Risk Factors
- Overweight or obesity, particularly abdominal fat
- Physical inactivity
- Family history of diabetes
- Age over 45
- History of gestational diabetes
- High blood pressure or abnormal cholesterol levels

## Self-Care Guidance
- Maintain a balanced diet low in refined sugar and processed carbohydrates
- Engage in regular physical activity (at least 150 minutes per week)
- Monitor blood glucose levels regularly if already diagnosed
- Maintain a healthy body weight
- Avoid tobacco use

## When to See a Doctor
- Persistent thirst and urination lasting more than a few days
- Unexplained fatigue combined with blurred vision
- Family history of diabetes with any of the above symptoms
- Blood glucose reading above 126 mg/dL fasting, or above 200 mg/dL random

## When It's an Emergency
- Confusion, difficulty breathing, or fruity-smelling breath (signs of
  diabetic ketoacidosis)
- Blood glucose above 300 mg/dL with vomiting or severe dehydration
- Loss of consciousness or seizure 

## Typical Lab Findings
- Elevated fasting blood glucose (126 mg/dL or above on repeated testing)
- Elevated random blood glucose (200 mg/dL or above with symptoms)
- Elevated HbA1c (6.5% or above)
- Lipid abnormalities (elevated triglycerides, low HDL cholesterol) are
  commonly associated
