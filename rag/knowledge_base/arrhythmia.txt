# Disease: Arrhythmia
## Category: Cardiac
## Source: WHO Fact Sheet - Cardiovascular Diseases (who.int)

## Overview
Arrhythmia refers to an irregular heartbeat, where the heart beats too
fast, too slow, or with an irregular rhythm. Some arrhythmias are
harmless, while others can be serious and require prompt medical
evaluation.

## Common Symptoms
- Palpitations (fluttering or racing heartbeat)
- Dizziness or lightheadedness
- Chest discomfort or pain
- Shortness of breath
- Fatigue
- Swelling in the ankles or legs
- Fainting or near-fainting episodes

## Causes and Risk Factors
- Underlying heart disease
- High blood pressure
- Electrolyte imbalances
- Excessive caffeine, alcohol, or stimulant use
- Stress
- Thyroid disorders

## Self-Care Guidance
- Avoid excessive caffeine, alcohol, and stimulants
- Manage stress through relaxation techniques
- Take prescribed heart medications consistently
- Monitor and report new or worsening symptoms promptly

## When to See a Doctor
- Noticeable palpitations lasting more than a few minutes
- Recurring dizziness or lightheadedness
- New swelling in the legs or ankles

## When It's an Emergency
- Chest pain with palpitations
- Fainting or loss of consciousness
- Severe shortness of breath
- Very rapid or very slow heart rate with symptoms of distress
