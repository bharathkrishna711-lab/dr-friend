# Disease: Heart Failure
## Category: Cardiac
## Source: WHO Fact Sheet - Cardiovascular Diseases (who.int)

## Overview
Heart failure occurs when the heart cannot pump blood efficiently enough
to meet the body's needs. It is a serious, often chronic condition that
requires ongoing medical management, and any escalation of symptoms
should be treated with high caution.

## Common Symptoms
- Shortness of breath, especially when lying down or with exertion
- Swelling in the legs, ankles, or abdomen
- Persistent fatigue and weakness
- Rapid or irregular heartbeat
- Persistent cough or wheezing, sometimes with white or pink phlegm
- Sudden weight gain from fluid retention

## Causes and Risk Factors
- Coronary artery disease
- High blood pressure (long-term, uncontrolled)
- Prior heart attack
- Diabetes
- Arrhythmias

## Self-Care Guidance
- Monitor daily weight for sudden fluid retention
- Limit salt and fluid intake as advised by a doctor
- Take prescribed heart medications consistently
- Avoid excessive physical exertion beyond medical advice

## When to See a Doctor
- Increasing shortness of breath with usual activities
- New or worsening swelling in legs or abdomen
- Sudden unexplained weight gain

## When It's an Emergency
- Severe shortness of breath at rest
- Chest pain
- Coughing up pink, frothy phlegm
- Fainting or severe dizziness
