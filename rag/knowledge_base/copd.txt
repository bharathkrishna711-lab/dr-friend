# Disease: COPD
## Category: Respiratory
## Source: WHO Fact Sheet - Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease (who.int)

## Overview
Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease (COPD) is a long-term lung
condition that causes airflow blockage and breathing difficulties. It
is progressive and most commonly caused by long-term exposure to
irritants such as tobacco smoke.

## Common Symptoms
- Persistent cough, often with mucus
- Shortness of breath, especially during physical activity
- Wheezing
- Chest tightness
- Frequent respiratory infections
- Fatigue

## Causes and Risk Factors
- Long-term smoking (primary cause)
- Long-term exposure to air pollution or occupational dust/chemicals
- Genetic factors (rare)
- Age (more common over 40)

## Self-Care Guidance
- Avoid smoking and secondhand smoke completely
- Use prescribed inhalers or medications as directed
- Practice breathing exercises as advised by a doctor
- Stay physically active within tolerable limits
- Get vaccinated against flu and pneumonia if advised

## When to See a Doctor
- Increasing shortness of breath with daily activities
- Cough lasting more than a few weeks
- Frequent chest infections

## When It's an Emergency
- Severe difficulty breathing at rest
- Bluish tint to lips or fingernails
- Confusion or extreme drowsiness
- Rapid heartbeat with breathing difficulty
