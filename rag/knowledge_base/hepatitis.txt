# Disease: Hepatitis
## Category: Infectious
## Source: WHO Fact Sheet - Hepatitis A (who.int)

## Overview
Hepatitis is inflammation of the liver, commonly caused by viral
infection. Hepatitis A, spread through contaminated food and water, is
common in areas with limited access to clean water and sanitation,
including parts of India.

## Common Symptoms
- Fatigue
- Nausea and vomiting
- Abdominal pain, particularly upper right side
- Loss of appetite
- Yellowing of skin and eyes (jaundice)
- Dark urine and pale stools
- Mild fever

## Causes and Risk Factors
- Consuming contaminated food or water
- Poor sanitation and hygiene
- Close contact with an infected person
- Travel to areas with high hepatitis prevalence

## Self-Care Guidance
- Rest and stay well hydrated
- Avoid alcohol completely during recovery
- Eat small, light, easily digestible meals
- Maintain good hand hygiene to avoid spreading infection

## When to See a Doctor
- Yellowing of skin or eyes
- Persistent nausea, vomiting, or abdominal pain
- Dark urine or pale stools

## When It's an Emergency
- Confusion or altered mental state
- Severe abdominal pain
- Easy bruising or bleeding
- Persistent vomiting preventing fluid intake

## Typical Lab Findings
- Markedly elevated liver enzymes (SGPT/ALT, SGOT/AST), often several
  times the upper normal limit
- Elevated bilirubin, correlating with jaundice
- Mildly low serum albumin in some cases
- White blood cell count usually normal
